Privacy

Your email, plainly

The release list exists so we can tell you when Lodestar Island is ready. Here is everything it keeps.

What we store

  • Your email address.
  • When you first signed up, when you confirmed, and when you last changed your choices.
  • Your choices: open beta notice yes/no and closed beta invite request yes/no. Everyone gets the full-release notice.
  • A reserved username, if you chose one.
  • Which announcements we've already sent you, so nobody gets one twice.
  • For about two hours, a scrambled (hashed) form of your network address, used only to slow down abuse. We never keep the address itself.

What we send

A confirmation email when you sign up, a link when you ask for one, and the announcements you chose: the full release, plus the open beta and closed beta invites if you asked. Very occasionally we may send a correction to an announcement you received. Nothing else.

Where it lives

In our own Amazon Web Services account: an Amazon DynamoDB table for storage and Amazon SES for sending. We don't use a mailing-list company or any tracking. Your address is never sold, rented or shared.

How long we keep it

  • Unconfirmed signups are deleted automatically after about a week.
  • Confirmed signups are kept until you unsubscribe or we close the list.
  • Reserved usernames are held until 180 days after the full-release email. To claim yours, create a game account with the same email address.
  • Unsubscribing deletes your record immediately. Our database's recovery backups, kept only for disaster recovery, roll off within 35 days.

Your choices

Every email we send includes a link to change your choices or unsubscribe. You can also request your link here.
